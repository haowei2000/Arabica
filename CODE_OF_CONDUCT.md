# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

We want Structure to be a practical, respectful project for maintainers,
contributors, and users. Expected behavior includes:

- Being direct and constructive.
- Respecting different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Keeping technical disagreement focused on facts, tradeoffs, and project goals.
- Giving and receiving feedback in good faith.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory
language, doxxing, sustained disruption, or publishing private information
without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, block users, or take other
reasonable action when behavior harms the project or its contributors.

Report conduct issues privately through the maintainer contact listed on the
repository profile. Include relevant links, screenshots, and context when
possible.
